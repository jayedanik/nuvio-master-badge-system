Questions, answered.

Why pre-render every badge at 96px native WebP?

Nuvio never scales oversized SVGs or heavy PNGs on the fly. Each badge averages ~6.8 KB, caches instantly in memory, and keeps scrolling locked at 60/120 FPS.

Are all badge backgrounds 100% transparent?

Yes. Every badge across all 24 themes uses a clean alpha channel (#00000000) with zero black boxes or rectangular artifacts.

What makes Live TV & Sports Broadcast different?

It is a dedicated borderless typography theme built exclusively for IPTV, Live TV, and Sports streams while keeping the iconic ON AIR LIVE mark untouched.

Can I share a direct link to a specific theme?

Yes. Opening any theme's Inspector updates the URL hash (e.g. #theme=gta6-vice-sunset) for instant bookmarking or sharing.
